For schools and partners

We run our programs with partners: schools that host an extracurricular, organisations that already work with children, and municipalities that want the programs for their own teachers. We are looking for places to practice and people to practice with, not funding.

Schools

A fellow runs an after-hours club with your children during the practice weeks. You give a room, a group, and a contact person; we bring the program, the mentor, and the stipend.

Organisations that work with children

After-school programs, camps, and youth centres can host practice weeks and gain a trained teacher for a season.

Municipalities and networks

Bring the programs to your own teachers and coordinators, cohort by cohort, with practice in your schools.
